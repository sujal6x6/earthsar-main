Privacy Policy

This policy explains how earthsar handles information shared through this website and during real estate advisory enquiries.

earthsar may collect details such as name, phone number, email address, enquiry topic, message, review content and approved media submitted by clients. This information is used to respond to enquiries, provide property advisory support, manage client communication, improve the website and display approved testimonials or media where permission has been given.

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with trusted service providers or advisors when needed to respond to a request, operate the website, manage a property enquiry or meet legal requirements.

Client review photos or videos are published only when they are submitted for that purpose or approved for public display. If you want a review, image or video removed, contact earthsar using the details below.

Reasonable steps are taken to protect submitted information, but no internet system can be guaranteed completely secure. By using this website, you agree that earthsar may handle your information as described here.
