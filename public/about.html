About earthsar

Real estate advisory built on honesty, transparency and trust.

earthsar is a Gurugram based real estate advisory firm helping people make clearer property decisions across Gurugram and Delhi NCR.

Who we are

earthsar works with home buyers, investors, business owners and families who want practical real estate advice before they commit to a property decision. Our role is to understand the client's requirement, explain options clearly, compare projects and help with the next steps from shortlisting to negotiation and documentation support.

The company is led by real estate professionals with long experience in residential and commercial property advisory, sales leadership, market understanding and client relationship management. Instead of pushing a listing first, the team focuses on the client's purpose, budget, location preference, timeline and risk comfort.

Our work is grounded in three core values: honesty, transparency and trust. That means clear conversations, realistic guidance and a relationship-first approach for property decisions in Gurugram, Delhi NCR, Noida, Greater Noida and Delhi.

What clients come to us for

  • Residential property buying advice for end-use and family homes.
  • Investment advisory with a focus on suitability, location and long-term value.
  • Commercial real estate guidance for offices, retail and business spaces.
  • Selling and leasing support with negotiation and process guidance.
  • Documentation coordination and post-deal support.
