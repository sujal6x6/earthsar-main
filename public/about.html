About earthsar

One advisory firm for property decisions built on honesty, transparency and trust.

earthsar is a Gurugram based real estate advisory firm helping home buyers, investors, business owners and families make clearer decisions across Gurugram and Delhi NCR.

Talk to earthsarMeet the team

An advisory firm, not a listings portal.

Buying, selling or investing in property is one of the largest financial decisions most people make. earthsar exists to make that decision clearer. We start with your goals, study the market and the paperwork, and give you an honest recommendation, including when the answer is to wait.

Our role is to understand the client's requirement, explain options clearly, compare projects and help with the next steps from shortlisting to negotiation and documentation support. The work is practical, relationship-led and focused on long-term trust rather than a quick transaction.

What earthsar stands for

Honesty

Clear advice, realistic expectations and no unnecessary pressure.

Transparency

Plain-language conversations about property options, risks, paperwork and market context.

Trust

Relationships that continue beyond a single site visit, booking or transaction.

What clients come to us for

  • Residential property buying advice for end-use and family homes.
  • Investment advisory with a focus on suitability, location and long-term value.
  • Commercial real estate guidance for offices, retail and business spaces.
  • Selling and leasing support with negotiation and process guidance.
  • Documentation coordination and post-deal support.

The earthsar promise

We are not here simply to show you properties. We are here to advise you through real estate decisions you can trust.
